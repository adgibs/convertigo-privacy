Privacy Policy

Convertigo — Engineering Unit Converter

Last Updated: February 24, 2026

Convertigo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Overview

Convertigo is developed by Andrew G. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

What We Don't Collect

  • No account or login required
  • No usage analytics collected
  • No third-party tracking services
  • No access to contacts, location, or photos
  • No internet connection required for core functionality

Data Stored on Your Device

All app data stays entirely on your device:

  • Conversion History — stored locally only
  • Favourites — stored locally only
  • Custom Conversions — stored locally only
  • Theme Preference — stored locally only

This data is never transmitted to any server or third party.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (Convertigo Pro and tip jar items) processed entirely through Apple's App Store. Convertigo does not receive, store, or have access to any payment information. All transactions are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

The App communicates with the Apple App Store solely for the purpose of verifying in-app purchases via StoreKit. No other third-party services are used.

Children's Privacy

Convertigo does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy
